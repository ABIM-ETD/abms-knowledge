# Don’t Digitize Yesterday, Design for Tomorrow

**Tuesday, 2026-09-15** · 4:30 PM - 5:30 PM CST

## Speakers

- [[Heather Honeycutt, MBA, MPM]] — Vice President, Organizational Effectiveness, American Board of Pediatrics
- [[Amy Hodak, CPMSM]] — Director, MOC Administration, American Board of Pediatrics

## Description

The American Board of Pediatrics (ABP) is undertaking a multi‑phased business transformation to modernize how we support certification, assessment, and continuous learning. The ABP intentionally structured its work as a phased journey—beginning with a proof of concept with a Commercial Off‑the‑Shelf (COTS) product, progressing through a rigorous pre‑discovery phase, and culminating in a formal discovery effort that resulted in a buildable business transformation plan.

Using ABP’s experience as a case study, this session will highlight lessons learned about sequencing work, managing change, engaging stakeholders, and building alignment to move confidently from exploration to execution.
