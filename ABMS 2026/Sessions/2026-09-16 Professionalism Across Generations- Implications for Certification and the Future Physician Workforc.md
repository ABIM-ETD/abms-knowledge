# Professionalism Across Generations: Implications for Certification and the Future Physician Workforce

**Wednesday, 2026-09-16** · 11:00 AM - 12:00 PM CST

**Topic:** [[Board Certification and Professionalism]], [[Physician Workforce]]

## Speakers

- [[Angela Macauley, PMP]] — President/CEO, NBCOT, Maryland
- [[Margaret A. Bent (she-her-hers)]] — Snr VP Competency Assessment, National Board for Certification in Occupational Therapy , Maryland

## Description

Health professions are experiencing significant generational shifts as younger physicians enter training and practice with different expectations around communication, hierarchy, feedback, work–life integration, and professional identity. These shifts may influence how professionalism is understood, expressed, and assessed across the medical education continuum.

Certification organizations and specialty boards play an important role in defining and reinforcing professional standards that support patient trust, safe care, and effective team-based practice.

This interactive session will explore how generational differences may influence professional norms and expectations in health care and consider implications for certification programs. Through real-time audience polling, facilitated discussion, and scenario-based reflection, participants will examine examples of evolving communication styles, workplace expectations, and emerging questions related to digital professionalism and professional conduct.

Attendees will consider how certification organizations can uphold core standards of professionalism while remaining responsive to generational change. The session will conclude with practical considerations for boards seeking to support professionalism education, guidance, and assessment across the workforce.Learning Objectives: Describe generational trends influencing professional expectations among emerging professionals entering the workforce.Examine how generational shifts may affect professionalism standards and assessment in certification programs.Identify strategies certification organizations can use to reinforce professionalism across generations.
