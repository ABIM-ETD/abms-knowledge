# New and Emerging Areas in Medicine: Competencies for an AI-Enabled Practice

**Thursday, 2026-09-17** · 9:15 AM - 10:15 AM CST

**Topic:** [[Artificial Intelligence]], [[Research and Innovations]], [[Physician Workforce]]

## Speakers

- [[Alison Whelan, MD MD]] — Chief Academic Officer, Association of American Medical Colleges, District of Columbia

## Description

Artificial intelligence (AI) is rapidly reshaping clinical care, assessment, and practice, creating new expectations for physicians’ competencies across the continuum of education and certification. In response, the Association of American Medical Colleges (AAMC), with support from the Josiah Macy Jr Foundation, has developed a national framework of AI competencies as part of its New and Emerging Areas in Medicine initiative.

These competencies, developed through a systematic inclusive process, are designed to align the continuum of education (from med school graduate to experienced physician) and aim to prepare physicians to use, evaluate, and collaborate with tools and technologies while attending to ethical, legal, and professional implications of AI-enabled care.

This interactive session will introduce the new AAMC AI competencies and explore their implications for specialty boards, certification standards, and lifelong learning. Participants will examine how these outcomes intersect with existing competency frameworks, assessment strategies, and evolving expectations for physician practice.

Through facilitated dialogue and case-based discussion, attendees will consider how certification organizations can help ensure consistent expectations for AI literacy, professional judgment, and safe integration of AI into clinical decision-making.

Agenda will include 2 parts (Introduction to AAMC AI competency development process and the new framework; Certification and assessment implications including an interactive application exercise). Guest facilitators during this session will include those who serve on the project Advisory Committee or Working Group listed here: https://www.aamc.org/aiLearning Objectives: Describe the goals and structure of the AAMC AI competencies within the New and Emerging Areas in Medicine competency series.Examine implications of AI competencies for certification, assessment, and ongoing professional development across specialties.Identify strategies for specialty boards to incorporate AI-related knowledge, skills, and professional behaviors into standards and assessment approaches.
