# Building an AI-Powered Innovation Team: Using a Knowledge Base and LLM to Supercharge Human Ability

**Thursday, 2026-09-17** · 4:30 PM - 5:30 PM CST

**Topic:** [[Artificial Intelligence]]

## Speakers

- [[Zak Barbezat (he-him-his)]] — Software Engineer, American Board of Internal Medicine, Pennsylvania
- [[David Solot, PhD (he-him-his)]] — Manager, Innovation Science, American Board of Internal Medicine, Pennsylvania

## Description

A knowledge base makes your team's institutional knowledge organized and accessible. A large language model makes your team faster, more creative, and better at synthesizing information. Each tool delivers real value on its own, but when combined, the result is more powerful than either alone.

ABIM's Emerging Technology team will demonstrate how pairing a structured knowledge base with an LLM creates an organizational intelligence layer that fundamentally amplifies what your team can do. When the LLM has persistent, structured access to your projects, people, decisions, deadlines, and research history, it stops being a general-purpose tool and becomes a context-aware resource tailored to your team's priorities, history, and ongoing decisions.

The result is a team that produces more and operates at a higher level, without losing sight of the work. The structured knowledge base means AI activity is visible, traceable, and easy to review. The humans on the team remain focused on decisions, priorities, and judgment, freed from the friction that previously slowed them down.

This is not a theoretical framework. It is the system ABIM's innovation team runs on today.
Attendees will see live demonstrations of AI-assisted project management, meeting preparation, action item tracking, research synthesis, and hands-on technical work. They will leave with a replicable blueprint they can apply immediately, using tools that are free or low-cost.Learning Objectives: Explain how pairing a knowledge management system (Obsidian) with an LLM (Claude) creates persistent organizational intelligence that improves AI utility.Design a basic framework for organizing knowledge in a way that maximizes the effectiveness of AI tools, applicable to innovation teams, departments, or individuals.Demonstrate at least two practical applications of AI-assisted knowledge management that can be implemented immediately within their own organization or team.
