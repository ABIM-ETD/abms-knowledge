# EXHIBITOR SHOWCASE presented by ITS | Breakfast Served

**Thursday, 2026-09-17** · 8:00 AM - 9:00 AM CST

## Description

From Practice to Security: Building a Modern Medical Assessment Ecosystem

Medical certification programs are under growing pressure to modernize assessment delivery, strengthen exam security, and better support candidates throughout the certification journey—all while adapting to rapidly evolving technologies and rising expectations.

Join ITS for a forward-looking session on how organizations are building connected assessment ecosystems that support candidate readiness, flexible test delivery, and lifelong learning. We’ll explore practical strategies for leveraging high-fidelity practice experiences, formative in-training exams (ITEs), secure remote proctoring, and evidence-based longitudinal assessment models to improve flexibility, enhance candidate engagement, and maintain confidence in exam integrity. This session will also highlight emerging innovations in assessment design, including AI-driven avatar item types that create a more realistic, interactive candidate experience.

Attendees will gain insight into how modern certification programs are moving toward integrated, scalable approaches that support operational goals while delivering more adaptive, future-ready experiences for today’s medical professionals.

Showcase will run for 30 minutes, from 8:15 - 8:45 am
