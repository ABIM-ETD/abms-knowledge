# Advancing Continuing Certification through Article-Based Assessment

**Friday, 2026-09-18** · 9:45 AM - 10:45 AM CST

**Topic:** [[Artificial Intelligence]], [[Research and Innovations]]

## Speakers

- [[Huaping Sun, PhD, MBA]] — Vice President, Test Development, American Board of Psychiatry & Neurology, Illinois
- [[Kevin Joldersma, PhD]] — Principal Psychometrician, American Board of Emergency Medicine, Michigan
- [[David D. Shin, PhD]] — VP, Research and Psychometrics, American Board of Psychiatry & Neurology, Illinois

## Description

Article-based assessment is an innovative approach to evaluating physicians’ engagement with literature while supporting lifelong learning. In this model, physicians respond to questions tied to specific journal articles or synopses of new/evolving literature, demonstrating comprehension, critical analysis, and the ability to apply new knowledge to clinical practice.

Three American Board of Medical Specialties Member Boards—American Board of Emergency Medicine (ABEM), American Board of Obstetrics and Gynecology (ABOG), and American Board of Psychiatry and Neurology (ABPN)—have incorporated article-based assessment into their continuing certification programs. Although they share a common goal, each board has developed a distinct implementation strategy.

In this session, representatives from these boards will discuss their program designs, guiding principles, and operational frameworks, including article/content curation, question development, psychometric considerations, and approaches to balancing standardization with personalization. They will also review analyses of user feedback, perceived relevance, and overall experience.

ABEM will describe its approach to translating rapidly evolving medical advances into accessible assessment content; ABOG will present findings from a research study examining the accuracy of large language models in answering article-based questions; and ABPN will share analyses of item performance by article and question type, physician subgroup performance, and survey results related to utility and satisfaction. Panelists will conclude by sharing lessons learned from pilot testing and operational rollout, highlighting successes and opportunities for improvement.Learning Objectives: describe the purposes and core features of article-based assessment as a component for continuing certification programscompare and contrast how different ABMS Member Boards design and implement their article-based assessmentidentify key lessons and insights learned from implementing article-based assessment
