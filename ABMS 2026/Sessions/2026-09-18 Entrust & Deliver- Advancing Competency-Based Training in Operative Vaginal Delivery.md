# Entrust & Deliver: Advancing Competency-Based Training in Operative Vaginal Delivery

**Friday, 2026-09-18** · 8:30 AM - 9:30 AM CST

**Topic:** [[Research and Innovations]]

## Speakers

- [[Alexandria Kraus, MD, MMSc (she-her-hers)]] — Associate Fellowship Director - Maternal Fetal Medicine, University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, North Carolina
- [[Elizabeth Stanley, MD]] — Assistant Professor, University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill
- [[Audra Williams, MD, MPH]] — Residency Program Director - Obstetrics and Gynecology, University of Alabama at Birmingham

## Description

Entrustable Professional Activities (EPAs) are central to competency-based medical education (CBME), translating competencies into the observable clinical tasks physicians perform in practice. Yet many specialty certification organizations remain early in the process of developing and implementing EPAs in meaningful ways. This interactive session uses the development of an Operative Vaginal Delivery (OVD)-specific EPA in Obstetrics and Gynecology as a case study to explore practical strategies for EPA design, piloting, and implementation that apply across specialties.

Panelists will briefly present the rationale and development process for the OVD-specific EPA, including the use of expert consensus methods to define essential tasks as well as the creation of a supervisory-scale-based entrustment rubric. The OVD-specific EPA will serve as an example of how complex procedural and clinical decision-making activities can be operationalized within CBME frameworks.

Participants will work in small groups to consider the scope of their specialty, identify clinical activities that could be operationalized as EPAs, and discuss strategies for EPA development, piloting, and implementation across training programs and certification systems. Group work will analyze strategies to address common EPA implementation challenges, including assessor alignment in entrustment decisions, integration of workplace-based assessments into clinical workflow, and mitigation of bias in EPA assessment.

This session is designed for educators at any stage of the EPA journey and aims to foster cross-specialty collaboration to advance competency-based assessment across the medical education continuum.Learning Objectives: Upon completion, participants will be able to describe the key steps in designing entrustable professional activities (EPAs).Upon completion, participants will be able to identify approaches for piloting and integrating EPAs within training programs and certification systems.Upon completion, participants will be able to identify barriers to EPA implementation and apply strategies to promote equitable, unbiased assessment.
