# From Learner to Leader: Comparing Approaches to Professionalism Lapses Across Students, Residents and Physicians in Practice

**Wednesday, 2026-09-16** · 1:30 PM - 2:30 PM CST

**Topic:** [[Board Certification and Professionalism]]

## Speakers

- [[Stacey R. Rose, MD, FACP, FIDSA]] — Director, Center for Professionalism; Associate Professor of Medicine-Infectious Diseases, Baylor College of Medicine, Texas
- [[Steve Caloudas, PhD]] — Director, Integrated Student Support; Assistant Professor, Dept of Psychiatry & Behavioral Sciences, Baylor College of Medicine
- [[Bani M. Ratan, MD, EdD (she-her-hers)]] — Director of Educational Initiatives for Graduate Medical Education; Associate Professor of OBGYN, Baylor College of Medicine, Texas
- [[Betsy Williams, PhD, MPH (she-her-hers)]] — Clinical Program Director, Professional Renewal Center; Clinical Associate Professor of Psychiatry, School of Medicine, University of Kansas, Kansas

## Description

Professionalism lapses during training are associated with subsequent challenges including board certification. Although numerous remediation strategies have been described, most programs focus on a single group (e.g., students, residents, or faculty). This interactive panel will explore professionalism remediation in undergraduate medical education (UME), graduate medical education (GME), and among physicians in practice to identify shared principles, highlight meaningful differences, and elevate best practices across career stages.

Intro (10 min)
Speakers will review the negative impacts of unprofessional behavior on patient care and summarize evidence linking professionalism lapses in training to subsequent challenges with board certification and maintenance.

Interactive presentation (20 min)
Speakers with expertise in professionalism remediation at the UME, GME and faculty levels will each describe their program / approach, including assessing the context in which the lapse occurred, the competency framework used to set and measure professionalism expectations, and remediation strategies for each setting. Audience response questions will be used to prompt participant engagement.

Discussion (20 min)
In small groups, participants will discuss examples of professionalism lapses among students, residents and practicing physicians to identify similarities and differences in addressing the concern based on the individual’s role.

Report out and wrap up (10 min): Participants will share insights with the larger group, facilitating synthesis of best practices in professionalism remediation that are applicable across the continuum of training and practice. Speakers will close with take home points and Q&A.Learning Objectives: Describe the link between early professionalism lapses and later certification or practice challenges.Analyze how contextual assessment and competency frameworks guide remediation decisions for students, residents and practicing physiciansApply cross-continuum insights to strengthen professionalism oversight and remediation policy
