# EXHIBITOR SHOWCASE presented by ROC-P | Lunch Served

**Thursday, 2026-09-17** · 12:30 PM - 1:00 PM CST

## Description

Two Moving Targets: Keeping Certification Standards and Operations in Sync in a Changing Workforce

Specialty boards have never had the luxury of standing still. On one side, science evolves, workforce pipelines shift, and the definition of competency keeps getting refined. On the other, the technology underneath certification operations has cycled through on-premise systems, hosted solutions, cloud platforms, build-your-own attempts, and now AI - each transition promising relief and delivering its own complexity. Boards have been absorbing both kinds of change simultaneously, often with the same lean team. This session names that compounding pressure honestly and explores how certification leaders are building operations that can move with both targets - without starting over every time one of them shifts. Walk away with a practical framework for assessing whether your current setup is keeping pace or quietly falling behind.

The presentation will begin at 12:30pm for approximately 30 minutes.
