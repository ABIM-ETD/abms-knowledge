# The Importance of Psychological Safety for Today's Member Board Community Workforce

**Tuesday, 2026-09-15** · 4:30 PM - 5:30 PM CST

## Speakers

- [[Laura Castillo-Page, PhD]] — Chief People and Culture Officer, American Board of Medical Specialties
- [[Jennifer Hopp, SPHR, SHRM-CP, CDE (she-her-hers)]] — Director, Human Resources, American Board of Anesthesiology, North Carolina
- [[Paul Zobitz, MBA]] — Director of Operations, American Board of Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation, Minnesota
- [[Christine Tierney, MSN, RN, FACHE, SHRM-SCP, SPHR, CHCR]] — Vice President of Talent and Culture, American Board of Internal Medicine

## Description

In today’s evolving workplace, psychological safety is essential for fostering high-performing, innovative, and inclusive teams. This session will explore why psychological safety matters for medical organizations, particularly as equity and inclusion practitioners and underrepresented staff navigate increasing scrutiny and a persistent “safety gap.” Attendees will examine how environments that support open dialogue and authenticity lead to stronger collaboration, better performance outcomes, and more equitable workplaces. Through real-world examples, presenters will highlight both the benefits of cultivating psychological safety and the risks when it is absent.
