# Shaping the Future of Residency Education: Opportunities to Redefine Assessment and Competency Evaluation

**Tuesday, 2026-09-15** · 3:15 PM - 4:15 PM CST

## Speakers

- [[Brenda J. Nuncio Lujano, MS]] — Program Coordinator, Innovation and Competency-Based Education, American Board of Pediatrics, North Carolina
- [[Shannon Lamkin]] — Senior Resident & Program Coordinator, American Board of Otolaryngology - Head and Neck Surgery
- [[Michael Nelson, MD, PhD]] — President, American Board of Allergy and Immunology

## Description

Residency education is rapidly evolving, challenging traditional approaches to assessment and competency evaluation. This interactive discussion will explore the future of residency training, emerging trends in competency-based medical education, and how Member Boards are adapting to changing educational needs and expectations. Participants will discuss current challenges in resident assessment, share insights and initiatives, and explore practical approaches to preparing residents for independent practice in the years ahead.
