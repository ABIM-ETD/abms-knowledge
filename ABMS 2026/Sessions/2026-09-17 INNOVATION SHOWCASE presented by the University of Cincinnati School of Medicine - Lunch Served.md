# INNOVATION SHOWCASE presented by the University of Cincinnati School of Medicine | Lunch Served

**Thursday, 2026-09-17** · 12:00 PM - 12:30 PM CST

## Description

2-Sigma Reasoning: AI-Powered Precision Education for Clinical Decision Making

2-Sigma Reasoning is an AI virtual patient platform developed at the University of Cincinnati College of Medicine with support from the ABIM Foundation. Learners interview a simulated patient in natural conversation, take a history, examine, order tests, commit to a differential, and manage the case. The patient responds to their decisions, so learners can explore the different approaches to clinical reasoning or managing a patient, something a written case cannot offer. An AI supervisor provides individualized feedback on their reasoning after every encounter, with no faculty proctoring required, so learners get autonomy, repetition, and low-stakes deliberate practice on demand. Each encounter also generates detailed data on how clinicians reason, supporting a research program that maps reasoning pathways across the continuum from medical students to practicing diplomates. The platform reflects four years of development, testing, and refinement, with four research papers published or under review. Attendees can work through a live case and learn how their board communities can participate in the study.

The presentation will begin at 12:00pm for approximately 30 minutes.
