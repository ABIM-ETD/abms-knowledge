# ABMS and Board Certification 101: Understanding the Fundamentals | Breakfast Served

**Thursday, 2026-09-17** · 8:00 AM - 9:00 AM CST

**Topic:** [[Board Certification and Professionalism]], [[Emerging Topics]]

## Speakers

- [[Rich Waters, MBA, CAE]] — Chief Marketing and Communications Officer, American Board of Medical Specialties, Illinois

## Description

This session is for anyone who wants to better understand board certification—whether you’re brand new to the topic or you work with certification programs every day. We’ll take a step back and look at the big picture: what board certification is, why it matters, and how it fits into today’s health care system. You’ll hear about board certification from multiple perspectives, including physicians, hospitals, and the public. We’ll cover the history of board certification, how it is valued and used, and what goes into a credible certification program. From standards and subspecialties to research and public trust, you’ll leave with a clearer understanding of how certification works and the important role the American Board of Medical Specialties plays in supporting quality and trust in health care.

Breakfast will be served.Learning Objectives: Explain the role of board certification in health careDescribe why board certification matters to physicians, health systems, and the publicIdentify the core components of the board certification process
