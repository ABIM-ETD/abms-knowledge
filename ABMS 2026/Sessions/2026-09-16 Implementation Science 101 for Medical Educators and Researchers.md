# Implementation Science 101 for Medical Educators and Researchers

**Wednesday, 2026-09-16** · 3:00 PM - 4:00 PM CST

**Topic:** [[Research and Innovations]]

## Speakers

- [[Rebecca Williams, MD, PhD, MEdPsych]] — Assistant Professor, Northwestern University
- [[Kelli Scott, PhD]] — Assistant Professor, Northwestern University

## Description

Implementation Science (IS) is the scientific study of methods and strategies that facilitate the uptake of evidence-based practice and research into regular use. IS methods and frameworks have been used extensively to identify influences on health professional behavior related to the implementation of evidence-based recommendations for patient care. IS seeks to systematically close the gap between what we know and what we do. In very non-scientific language, IS is a method of understanding how best to help people ‘Do the Thing’ we want them to do. As a field, IS has been underutilized as a tool for improving medical education. Applying IS frameworks and principles to surgical education has the potential to facilitate the successful enactment of new educational practices by examining individual and systems level factors that impact behavior.

Session Format: This session is designed to be an interactive experience. Attendees will be engaged in small group work with rotating facilitators. There will be 3 main themes within the workshop: (1) Introduction to Implementation Science theories and frameworks, (2) Examples of Implementation Science in medical education, and (3) Facilitated application of an Implementation Science framework to an institution-specific problem.

Participants will be provided with handouts to provide a framework for planning and discussion. Rotating subject matter experts (workshop speakers) will serve as facilitators during the process. At the conclusion of the workshop, participants will have a framework they can use to address an implementation challenge they are facing.Learning Objectives: Discuss what is meant by Implementation Science.Describe two Implementation Science frameworks.Demonstrate how to use an Implementation Science framework to address an implementation challenge in medical education.
