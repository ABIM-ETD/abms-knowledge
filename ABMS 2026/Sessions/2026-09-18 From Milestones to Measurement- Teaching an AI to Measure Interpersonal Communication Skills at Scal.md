# From Milestones to Measurement: Teaching an AI to Measure Interpersonal Communication Skills at Scale

**Friday, 2026-09-18** · 9:45 AM - 10:45 AM CST

**Topic:** [[Research and Innovations]]

## Speakers

- [[David Solot, PhD (he-him-his)]] — Manager, Innovation Science, American Board of Internal Medicine, Pennsylvania
- [[Jim McAdams, MBA (he-him-his)]] — Senior Director, Innovation Strategy and Solutions, American Board of Internal Medicine, Pennsylvania
- [[Zak Barbezat (he-him-his)]] — Software Engineer, American Board of Internal Medicine, Pennsylvania

## Description

Measuring physician competency beyond medical knowledge remains one of certification's most persistent challenges. Interpersonal communication skills are critical to patient outcomes yet notoriously difficult to assess at scale. This session presents a two-stage research program addressing that gap.

First, we describe the development of a unified physician competency model linking ACGME/ABMS competency domains to specialty-specific Milestones and the discrete, observable behaviors underlying each. This framework transforms abstract competency language into measurable behavioral indicators.

Second, we demonstrate how we trained a large language model (LLM) using the Calgary-Cambridge communication framework to analyze physician-patient transcripts — detecting whether specific interpersonal behaviors were present in real clinical encounters. This represents the first step toward scalable, automated communication assessment: moving from binary behavior detection toward quality rating at the behavior level.

Attendees will leave with a replicable framework for operationalizing competencies into measurable behaviors, and an understanding of how AI can serve as a scalable rater of communication skills — reducing dependence on direct observation and human rating burden.Learning Objectives: Translate specialty-specific Milestones into discrete, observable behaviors that can be operationalized for large-scale assessment across certification programs.Explain how LLMs can be trained to detect the presence of specific interpersonal communication behaviors in physician-patient transcripts using an established framework.Identify opportunities to apply AI-driven communication assessment within their certification programs as a scalable alternative to direct observation and human rating.
