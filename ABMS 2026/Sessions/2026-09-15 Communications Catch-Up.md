# Communications Catch-Up

**Tuesday, 2026-09-15** · 4:30 PM - 5:30 PM CST

## Speakers

- [[AJ Wolf]] — Director of Communications, American Board of Emergency Medicine, Michigan
- [[Bonnie Woodworth]] — Communications Director, American Board of Pathology
- [[Marie Teske]] — Customer Service Lead, American Board of Physical Medicine and Rehabilitation

## Description

Join fellow Member Board communicators for an interactive conversation on the ideas, challenges, and strategies shaping communications today. Share what is working in your organization, from using AI to improve productivity to navigating crisis communications, collaborating with support teams, and engaging new diplomates. You will leave with fresh perspectives, practical ideas, and valuable connections to carry into your next communication initiative.

Come ready to share, learn, and connect with peers across the Member Boards.
