# Expanding the Assessment Toolkit: A New Item Type for Clinical Reasoning and AI-Driven Operational Efficiency

**Wednesday, 2026-09-16** · 3:00 PM - 4:00 PM CST

**Topic:** [[Artificial Intelligence]]

## Speakers

- [[Ting Wang, PhD (she-her-hers)]] — Director of Assessment Innovation Research, American Board of Pediatrics, North Carolina
- [[Ying Du, PhD (she-her-hers)]] — Scoring Manager, American Board of Pediatrics, North Carolina

## Description

This session reviews two initiatives modernizing medical assessment through advanced psychometrics and AI.

The first introduces a framework for evaluating clinical reasoning under uncertainty using the Script Concordance Test (SCT). Unlike traditional formats relying on discrete correct answers, the SCT probes diagnostic reasoning in ambiguous, real-world scenarios — precisely where expert judgment matters most. A novel psychometric model scores physicians' ability to differentiate and weigh clinical data, moving beyond binary scoring to capture the full spectrum of expert reasoning. This reflects the recognition that clinical expertise is defined not by certainty, but by the capacity to navigate ambiguity with nuance. An AI-driven feedback layer complements this by replacing standardized critiques with personalized guidance — initiating reflective questions, addressing learner-specific responses, and targeting individualized growth areas.

The second initiative leverages AI to resolve systemic pain points in the assessment lifecycle. It focuses on establishing a structured roadmap for future AI initiatives and implementing low-resource projects for immediate application, shifting technical workflow burden away from our team to enable higher-level strategic focus. An AI multi-agent framework streamlines high-stakes exam production while enhancing quality control and scalability.

Together, these initiatives advance a cohesive agenda: reconceptualizing clinical competence in ambiguous diagnostic contexts, establishing a new paradigm for individualized feedback, and building operational infrastructure to drive productivity and scale rigorous medical certification.Learning Objectives: Review clinical decision-making under uncertainty by applying script concordance testing and a novel psychometric scoring modelDemonstrate personalized feedback leveraging a Large Language Model.Understand the steps to streamline operational processes of an assessment lifecycle.
