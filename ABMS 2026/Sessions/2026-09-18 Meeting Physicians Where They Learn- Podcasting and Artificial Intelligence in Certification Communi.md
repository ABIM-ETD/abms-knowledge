# Meeting Physicians Where They Learn: Podcasting and Artificial Intelligence in Certification Communication

**Friday, 2026-09-18** · 11:00 AM - 12:00 PM CST

**Topic:** [[Artificial Intelligence]], [[Emerging Topics]]

## Speakers

- [[Cassie North]] — Marketing and Engagement Specialist, American Board of Emergency Medicine, Michigan
- [[John T. Finnell, MD, MSc (he-him-his)]] — Director of Medical Affairs, American Board of Emergency Medicine, Indiana
- [[David Elstein]] — Manager, Education and Communication, American Board of Orthopaedic Surgery, North Carolina

## Description

Physicians, residents, and health care leaders face increasing demands on their time, making traditional communication methods less effective. Certification organizations must adapt how they share information to reach busy professionals through formats that fit into their daily routines.

This interactive panel will explore how the American Board of Emergency Medicine and the American Board of Orthopaedic Surgery have integrated podcasting into their communication strategies. Panelists will discuss how podcasts can translate complex information into concise, accessible audio content that listeners can engage with during commutes or other routine activities.

The session will also examine emerging technologies, including the use of AI tools to support podcast production. Panelists will discuss the benefits, limitations, and ethical considerations when using AI to support communication in the medical and certification environments.

Participants will be invited to share their own experiences with podcasts, audiocasts, and other digital communication channels. Through discussion and audience interaction, the session will explore how certification organizations can effectively deliver timely, credible information in an evolving digital media landscape.Learning Objectives: Identify strategies certification organizations can use to communicate with physicians through podcasting and other digital media.Understand tools you can use to support podcast production and communication workflows, including equipment, software, and AI.Evaluate opportunities and risks when using AI and audio platforms to share certification and medical information.
