# EXHIBITOR SHOWCASE presented by Intelligent Video Solutions | Breakfast Served

**Wednesday, 2026-09-16** · 8:00 AM - 8:30 AM CST

## Description

Capturing Competency: Secure Video Solutions for Assessment and Simulation

As certification programs lean more on OSCEs, simulation, and multi-site assessment, the way you capture and manage that video matters as much as the assessment itself. This session covers practical considerations for video-based assessment programs: keeping recordings secure, making footage searchable and usable for rater review and appeals, and scaling a system across multiple sites and rooms without adding IT burden. Drawing on real deployments in medical education and clinical simulation, we'll walk through what to look for in a video capture platform and lessons learned from programs already doing this well.

Showcase will run for 30 minutes, from 8:00 - 8:30 am
