# Recognition as a Strategy

**Thursday, 2026-09-17** · 10:45 AM - 11:45 AM CST

**Topic:** [[Emerging Topics]]

## Speakers

- [[Nicole Welk-Joerger, PhD (she-her-hers)]] — Program Manager, Stakeholder Engagement, American Board of Internal Medicine
- [[Lindsay Durkalec (she-her-hers)]] — Manager, Marketing and PR, ABIM
- [[Patrick Prante, MS (he-him-his)]] — Data Analyst, Assessment Research & Innovations, ABIM, Pennsylvania

## Description

While physicians set themselves apart in their commitment to continuing education and regular demonstration of their expertise, Certification can be seen as taxing for physicians and overlooked by patients. Fostering a sense of meaning and connection to the work of certification is a challenge in both communications and community-building. In 2023, ABIM centered physicians in this discussion and conducted qualitative research to understand what meaningful “recognition” of certification means to them.

This educational session will explore how “recognition as a strategy” can help build positive experiences with board certification for physicians. Presenters will outline how qualitative research informed three main recognition initiatives and their outcomes: personalized, data-driven emails highlighting individual achievements and milestones; a branded store offering free identity-affirming materials; and digital credentials that formally recognize their contributions.
