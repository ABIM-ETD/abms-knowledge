# The Next Era of Assessment — Innovating for the Future

**Wednesday, 2026-09-16** · 11:00 AM - 12:00 PM CST

**Topic:** [[Research and Innovations]]

## Speakers

- [[David A. Turner, MD (he-him-his)]] — Vice President, Innovation and Competency-Based Education, American Board of Pediatrics, North Carolina
- [[Cameron Wright, MD]] — Executive Director, American Board of Thoracic Surgery, Massachusetts
- [[Mohammed Minhaj, MD, MBA]] — Director, American Board of Anesthesiology

## Description

In this session, representatives from three boards will highlight current assessment innovation initiatives designed to reshape and enhance their certification programs.

First, the concept and importance of innovation in the Member Board community will be introduced. This section will focus on a brief description of how the American Board of Pediatrics’ (ABPeds’) current strategic plan is designed to advance assessment innovation as an organizational priority and enhance their certification program, including a description of facilitators and barriers to this work.

Next, the American Board of Anesthesiology (ABA) will describe its development of Resident Reach, a new longitudinal, formative assessment platform designed distinctly for residents and their program faculty. The Resident Reach platform will provide ongoing, low‑stakes assessment and feedback on medical knowledge, with the goal of supporting learning and skill development across the training continuum. Resident Reach is intended to spark discussion and coaching, it is not a question bank or an exam, and was inspired by the ABA’s MOCA Minute model.

Finally, experience piloting a key features testing paradigm in Thoracic Surgery (ABTS), based in part on the Canadian licensing system, will be reviewed. Data will be shared on this experience and how the learning led to changes to content development for the Thoracic Surgery oral exam. In addition, they will discuss their experience integrating AI into item generation.

Collectively, these presentations and the planned interactive panel discussion represents an opportunity to discuss innovative assessment efforts across three boards with substantially different sizes and resources and provide a framework for other boards to consider as they evolve their certification programs.Learning Objectives: Upon completion of this session, participant will be able to...Describe the concept of innovation and the importance of innovation for training programs and ABMS member boards.Upon completion of this session, participant will be able to....Discuss the value of a trainee engagement strategy and value of a longitudinal assessment program for learners.Upon completion of this session, participant will be able to...Explain the challenges and benefits of using Artificial Intelligence to facilitate generation of items for certification assessments.
