# Promotion in Place: A Roadmap for Competency-Based Time-Variable General Surgical Training to Promote Autonomy and Readiness for Independent Practice

**Thursday, 2026-09-17** · 9:15 AM - 10:15 AM CST

**Topic:** [[Research and Innovations]]

## Speakers

- [[Mary Ellen J. Goldhamer, MD MPH]] — Assistant Professor of Medicine, Harvard Medical School, Massachusetts General Hospital, Massachusetts
- [[Brenessa Lindeman, MD, MEHP, FACS (she-her-hers)]] — Professor of Surgery and Medical Education, Assistant Dean for GME, University of Alabama Birmingham Health System / School of Medicine, Alabama
- [[Jennifer LaFemina, MD, MAMSE, FACS (she-her-hers)]] — Professor of Surgery, Program Director, General Surgery Residency Program, University of Massachusetts Memorial Medical Center, Massachusetts

## Description

Competency-based medical education (CBME) is increasingly used as a framework for graduate medical education (GME). “Promotion in Place” (PIP) is a specific model of competency-based time-variable (CB-TV) GME, where qualified trainees who meet explicit competency criteria voluntarily graduate early from residency into a period of “sheltered independent” (PIP-SI) practice.

The PIP model has been approved for specific pilots by three certification boards: Pathology (2021), Obstetrics and Gynecology (2025), and Surgery (2026). Recruitment for a multi-center national pilot in general surgery training is underway, and a consortium of programs will together develop the process and structures necessary for adapting the PIP model to surgical training.

This session will report on the consortium discussions to date, noting perceived barriers and facilitators to PIP implementation, shared best practices, and considerations for the period of PIP-SI among participating programs. A series of PIP guides, customized for general surgical training, will be shared including process maps, a CCC simulation guide for competency-based determination of readiness for independent practice, and an FAQ. Entrustable Professional Activities (EPAs) and other metrics used to make competency determinations will be described.

This interactive session will invite questions and discussion regarding key considerations, challenges, and opportunities for programs, specialties, and Member Boards considering a CBTV-GME pilot.Learning Objectives: Provide an overview of the “Promotion in Place” CBTV-GME pilot and adaptations to general surgical trainingReview the explicit criteria that determines trainee qualification for early graduation, and a period of PIP sheltered independent practiceExplore the key considerations for planning schedules and practice activities for the period of “sheltered independent” practice.
