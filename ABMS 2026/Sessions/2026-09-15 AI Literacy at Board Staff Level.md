# AI Literacy at Board Staff Level

**Tuesday, 2026-09-15** · 3:15 PM - 4:15 PM CST

## Speakers

- [[Jennifer Hopp, SPHR, SHRM-CP, CDE (she-her-hers)]] — Director, Human Resources, American Board of Anesthesiology, North Carolina
- [[Chelsey Williams, MBA]] — Chief Operating Officer and Chief Financial Officer, American Board of Allergy and Immunology
- [[Brooke Houck, PhD (she-her-hers)]] — Psychometrician, The American Board of Radiology, North Carolina
- [[Lyndsay M. Tyler, M.P.H]] — Director, Strategic Initiatives, American Board of Emergency Medicine, Michigan

## Description

Artificial intelligence is becoming a more visible part of everyday work across Member Boards, and this session will provide a practical introduction to baseline AI concepts and staff training needs. Presenters will explore safe, useful staff-level applications such as drafting communications, summarizing content, supporting workflows, and fostering innovation and creativity. The discussion will also address governance and ethical considerations, including responsible use, potential risks, and what to avoid. Attendees will leave with a clearer understanding of how AI can support staff work thoughtfully, effectively, and responsibly.
