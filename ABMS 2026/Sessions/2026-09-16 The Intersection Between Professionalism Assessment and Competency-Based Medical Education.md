# The Intersection Between Professionalism Assessment and Competency-Based Medical Education

**Wednesday, 2026-09-16** · 3:00 PM - 4:00 PM CST

**Topic:** [[Board Certification and Professionalism]]

## Speakers

- [[Ariel Winn, MD]] — Assistant Professor, Associate Designated Institutional Official, Boston Children's Hospital/Harvard Medical School, Massachusetts
- [[Greg Ogrinc, MD, MS (he-him-his)]] — SVP, Certification Standards and Programs, American Board of Medical Specialties, Illinois
- [[Lisa Taitsman]] — Lisa Taitsman

## Description

In this session, representatives from ABMS and two specialties will discuss how competency-based medical education (CBME) can facilitate and enhance assessment of professionalism as part of training and certification.

First, a leader from ABMS will introduce principles underlying CBME and professionalism assessment and their role in certification. They will describe ABMS' development of two collaboratives, one focused on CBME and the other on promoting professionalism, and discuss successes, challenges, and opportunities for synergy across member boards.

Next, a pediatric education leader will discuss a large-scale CBME pilot implementing smartphone-based frontline assessment across general pediatrics residency programs, including intentional design of forms to assess professional behaviors such as communication, collaboration, and help-seeking, and share data on the platform's impact on professionalism assessment.

Finally, a leader from the American Board of Orthopaedic Surgery will describe their approach through the Knowledge, Skills, and Behaviors platform, a comprehensive competency assessment system grounded in CBME that includes professionalism assessments mirroring those used in certification decisions.

Collectively, these presentations and the subsequent panel discussion reinforce that CBME and professionalism assessment are deeply synergistic and offer a venue to discuss how specialties can advance both simultaneously.Learning Objectives: Describe how ABMS has fostered collaboration across member boards in CBME and professionalism and identify opportunities for synergy between these efforts.Discuss how the principles of CBME can facilitate assessment of professional behaviors in residency education to inform certification decisions.Explain how two specialties have implemented CBME-based professionalism assessment and compare their approaches to inform efforts in other specialties.
