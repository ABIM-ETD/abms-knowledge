# The Science of Certification: The Year’s Top 10 Certification Research Articles

**Wednesday, 2026-09-16** · 11:00 AM - 12:00 PM CST

**Topic:** [[Research and Innovations]]

## Speakers

- [[Martin V. Pusic, MD, PhD (he-him-his)]] — Director, ABMS Research and Education Foundation, Massachusetts

## Description

The quality and quantity of research into important aspects of certification is increasing exponentially. This is both a blessing and a curse for the Member Board community of practice. Blessing in that we can develop ever-more evidence-based systems. Curse in that we have to more rapidly appraise and monitor the evidence-base.

This workshop proposes to debate what research matters to ABMS and the Member Boards community, aiming to generate discussion that makes both researchers and practitioners better off.

The presenters will strive to animate a lively discussion around key examples of studies published within the last year or so. They will do this through both the careful selection of recent articles that have something to say, as well as through celebration of the diverse ways of knowing that can aid our mission.Learning Objectives: Promote evidence-based certification practices by reviewing recent examples of impactful, relevant research.Critically evaluate certification research whether it reinforces current practices or points the way to meaningful innovation and change.Exchange interdisciplinary knowledge in a forum where the research in one Member Board’s community can impact the processes in others.
