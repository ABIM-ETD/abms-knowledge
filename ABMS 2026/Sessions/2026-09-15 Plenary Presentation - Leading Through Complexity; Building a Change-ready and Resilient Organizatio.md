# Plenary Presentation - Leading Through Complexity; Building a Change-ready and Resilient Organization

**Tuesday, 2026-09-15** · 1:45 PM - 3:00 PM CST

## Speakers

- [[Lyndsay M. Tyler, M.P.H]] — Director, Strategic Initiatives, American Board of Emergency Medicine, Michigan
- [[Heather Honeycutt, MBA, MPM]] — Vice President, Organizational Effectiveness, American Board of Pediatrics

## Description

Member Boards are facing significant organizational changes that can affect strategy, operations, culture, and staff experience. This plenary presentation will explore how leaders can manage large-scale change while maintaining operational continuity and supporting employee resilience. Through examples and discussion, panelists will highlight practical approaches to communication, alignment, and workforce support during periods of transition. Attendees will gain insights they can apply in their own organizations to navigate change with greater clarity and care.
